Frequently asked questions


Do I need an account to buy something?

No. Pick "I'm a customer" on the home page and you can view the products and check out.

How does checkout work?

Press Buy on a product, enter the quantity and the cash you are handing over, then press Pay. You get a receipt with your change.

What does "Low stock" mean?

It means 5 or fewer of that item are left. When the stock reaches 0 the item is marked out of stock and can't be bought.

Why can't I restock or add products?

Only Inventory Admin and Supervisor accounts can restock and add products. Customers and cashiers can't.

Who can see the daily sales report?

Supervisor, Inventory Admin and Cashier accounts. Customers can't.

The staff login says the system is locked.

Three wrong tries lock the staff login. Restart the shop program to unlock it.

Why did my products disappear?

Products and sales are kept in memory while the server runs. When the server is restarted, everything goes back to the starting stock.
