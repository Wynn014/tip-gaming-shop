Terms & Conditions

Please read these before using the shop.


1. School project

TIP Gaming Shop is a student project made for Introduction to Computing. It is not a real store, and no real products are sold.

2. Accounts and roles

Staff accounts (Supervisor, Inventory Admin, Cashier) are for authorized users only. Access to features depends on the role. After 3 wrong login attempts the staff login is locked until you close the tab and open the site again.

3. Purchases

Prices are in Philippine pesos. A purchase goes through only when the item is in stock, the quantity is valid and the cash given covers the total. Stock goes down as soon as a purchase is completed.

4. Data

Products, stock and sales totals are saved in your own browser only. They are not shared with other visitors, and clearing your browser data resets them.

5. Changes

These terms may change as the project is updated.
